How to evaluate a business idea before investing

Start with the problem you want to solve, identify who experiences it, review existing solutions, and talk to potential customers. Before investing in a full operation, run a small test and record what you learn.

Market research: where to start

Define your market, understand customers, review competitors and alternatives, and record your information sources. Separate observed facts from assumptions. Early research should support concrete decisions about your offer.

What to include in an initial business plan

Organize your offer, customer, market, business model, operations, team, marketing, sales, and financial estimates. An initial plan should guide next decisions and evolve as you learn.
